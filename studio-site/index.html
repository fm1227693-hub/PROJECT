    About the studio

    Curious minds, obsessive makers

    Lusion is an independent creative production studio. We blend design, technology and storytelling into immersive real-time experiences — built by a small team of designers, engineers and 3D artists.

    BristolLondonWorldwide
    Philosophy

    The screen isa room you walkinto.

    We build experiences that behave like physical spaces. Light has weight. Type has depth. Motion has consequence. Every scroll is an input, not a page turn.

    Our pipeline is deliberately small: a designer, an engineer and a 3D artist on every project from day one. No handoffs, no loss of intent, no compromise between the render and the release.
