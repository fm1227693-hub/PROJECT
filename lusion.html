About the studio

Curious minds, obsessive makers

Lusion is an independent creative production studio. We blend design, technology and storytelling into immersive real-time experiences — built by a small team of designers, engineers and 3D artists.

BristolLondonWorldwide
What drives us
01

Craft over shortcuts

Every shader, easing curve and interaction is hand-tuned. We don't reach for templates — we build systems that fit the story.

02

Performance is design

A beautiful frame that stutters is a broken frame. We budget every millisecond and profile on real devices, not just top-tier laptops.

03

Play seriously

Our best work started as a Lab experiment. We protect time to explore, break things, and turn curiosity into client value.

04

Partners, not vendors

We embed with your team, share our process openly, and stay long after launch to keep the experience evolving.

The team

Small crew, big canvas.

Journey
2018

Founded in Bristol by two engineers and a designer with a shared obsession for real-time graphics on the web.

2020

First Awwwards Site of the Year nomination. The team grows to eight and moves into a converted warehouse studio.

2022

Launch of Lusion Labs — our open R&D playground for fluid sims, GPGPU particles and generative shaders.

2024

Studio of the Year. Work shipped for automotive, fashion and consumer tech brands across four continents.

2026

Pushing into spatial computing, WebGPU pipelines and real-time collaborative 3D — and still chasing 120fps.
